# ArcaneShortcuts v1.2.3

## New macros

### Warrior
- **Disarm** (General): switches to Defensive Stance if needed, then disarms your mouseover or target.
- **Shield Wall** (General): switches to Defensive Stance if needed, then uses Shield Wall.
- **Shield Block** (Protection): switches to Defensive Stance if needed, then uses Shield Block.
- **Stance Flip** (Protection): flips between Battle Stance and Defensive Stance.
- **Charge/Exe/Taunt** (Protection): Charge out of combat, then Execute in Battle Stance or Taunt in Defensive Stance.

### Druid
- **Mark + Thorns** (General): Mark of the Wild, then Thorns on the next press. Hold Shift for Gift of the Wild.
- **Bear Gear** (Feral Bear): enters Dire Bear Form and equips your tanking rings. Fill in your ring names.

### Hunter
- **Eagle Eye** (General): casts Eagle Eye at your cursor. Press it again while looking through it to scout further.

## Fixes
- The minimap button now sits on the edge of the minimap whatever its size, and follows square minimaps from minimap addons.

## Known issues
- Players report that the Warrior Vanguard talent currently breaks macros that use `/cast Charge`.
